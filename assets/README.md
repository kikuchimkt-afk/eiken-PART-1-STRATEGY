# 写真素材

`study-desk.webp` は、このアプリ用に built-in `image_gen` で作成した写真調の画像です。1024 × 1536 px、WebP形式に圧縮して使用しています。

## 生成プロンプト

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an editorial photograph to serve as the main background image of a Japanese teenage English learning app used only on smartphones. Portrait 2:3 composition. A quiet inviting study desk beside a large window, an open English study notebook with indistinct unreadable handwriting in the lower right, a cream closed book, a pencil and a small clear glass of water, soft foliage outside the window and one small green plant, warm late afternoon light, pale warm oak, neutral linen and muted sage colors, authentic subtle paper and wood textures. No people. The top half has softly out of focus window, greenery and gentle atmospheric light with generous negative space for a white title overlay. Bottom half shows the desk and study materials. Photographic quality, natural and sophisticated, calm and trustworthy educational feeling, restrained palette. No graphic design, no logos, no watermarks, no legible text, no UI, no borders.
